Trigger: a side agent warned that any `v*` tag publishes to nuget.org straight away. I checked, and it's right,
with one qualification.
- **Today a tag can't publish.** The repo had no environments, secrets or tags, so `NuGet/login` would fail.
- **The risk starts once the setup is done.** After the nuget.org policy and the `NUGET_USER` secret exist, a tag
  that passes the gates publishes. GitHub auto-creates a missing environment with no protection rules, and
  nuget.org versions can't be deleted, only unlisted.
